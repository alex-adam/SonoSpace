# SonoSpace

Design your perfect atmosphere with customizable ambient sounds built for relaxation, focus, sleep, and everyday calm.

Whether you want soothing rain for sleep, cozy vibes for studying, calming forest sounds for meditation, or your own unique mix of ambience layers — SonoSpace lets you create personalized soundscapes in seconds.

---

## Features

- Create custom soundscapes with up to 6 sound layers
- Adjust individual volume levels for every layer
- Choose from 45+ ambient sounds and combinations
- Includes 5 carefully crafted presets to get started instantly
- Built-in sleep timer for nighttime listening
- Fully offline — no internet connection required
- No account or sign-up required

Perfect for:

- Sleep & relaxation
- Studying & productivity
- Meditation & mindfulness
- Stress relief
- Reading & deep focus
- Creating cozy atmospheres

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does SonoSpace require an internet connection?

No. All sounds are bundled directly within the app and work completely offline.

### Does SonoSpace collect personal data?

No. SonoSpace does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

### Do I need to create an account?

No. The app does not require sign-up, login, or any type of account.

### Does the app contain ads?

No. SonoSpace currently does not contain advertisements.

### Are subscriptions required?

No. All current features are available for free. Optional premium features may be introduced in future versions.

### Can I use the app while the screen is locked?

Yes. Audio playback is designed to continue in the background.
